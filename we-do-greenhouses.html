Project Overview

We Do Greenhouses is a Vancouver-based greenhouse company, specializing in high-quality modular greenhouses and self-watering planter boxes for home gardeners and small-scale farmers.

In partnership with We Do Greenhouses, I collaborated with their team to create a distinctive brand identity. The logo design was inspired by the clean, geometric lines of their greenhouse structure, serving as a visual anchor for the brand. With a focus on connecting with novice gardeners, young families, and urban growers with limited space, the identity was crafted to feel grounded, timeless, and sophisticated, reflecting the brand’s commitment to sustainable and accessible growing solutions.
