Project Overview

Shroomie is a portable desktop video camera with the housing inspired by a mushroom.

The purpose of this assignment is to design and fabricate a functioning and aesthetically pleasing video-conferencing unit that will solve real-world problems. With this project, our team was tasked to apply a common and uncommon combination of materials to generate an aesthetically pleasing and functional model. My main role within this project was taking lead on fabrication of the camera and assisting with the initial design of the aspects of the concept of this project.
