About

Hi, my name is Taylen

I am a visual communication designer from Vancouver, British Columbia. My skills specialize in graphic design, photography and computer programming. Currently, I am a senior student at Simon Fraser University pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Interactive Arts & Technology.

My design practices are focused on creating projects with intriguing visual design narratives that showcase the human connection with one’s self, with others and the world. I have an eye for detail allows me to focus on the unique relationships created between each project and their audiences. I want to capture these emotions and create works with meaningful and impactful stories. My expertise in the Adobe Creative Suite with programs such as Photoshop, Lightroom, InDesign and Illustrator allows me to become the storyteller to showcase these stories. I want to help you tell your story. Let's collaborate!
