About

Hello, my name is Taylen

I am a graphic designer & photographer from Vancouver, British Columbia. I love to explore artistic mediums & projects that challenge my creativity. Currently I am pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Interactive Arts & Technology with a minor in Print & Digital Publishing at Simon Fraser University. My studies focus on visual design as well as computer programming.

My design practices are focused on creating projects with intriguing visual design narratives that showcase the human connection with one’s self, with others and the world. I have an eye for detail allows me to focus on the unique relationships created between each project and their audiences. I want to capture these emotions and create works with meaningful and impactful stories. My expertise in the Adobe Creative Suite with programs such as Photoshop, Lightroom, InDesign and Illustrator allows me to become the storyteller to showcase these stories. I believe every project has a meaningful story and an audience that's awaiting to listen. I want to help you tell your story. Let's collaborate!

Coded & created with tender loving care.
