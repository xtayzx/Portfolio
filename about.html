Hello, my name is Taylen.

I am a multidisciplinary visual designer who loves to explore and work on all things creative. Currently, I am pursuing my Bachelors in Interactive Arts and Technology with a minor in Print and Digital Publishing at Simon Fraser University. My studies are focused around Design as well as Interactive Systems.

My design practices focus on showcasing the human connection with one’s self, with others and the world. I want to capture these emotions and moments and create works that share meaningful and impactful stories. My expertise in the Adobe Creative Suite with programs such as Photoshop, Lightroom, InDesign and Illustator allow me to become the visual storyteller to showcase these authetic stories. I believe every project has a meaningful story to tell and an audience to listen. I want to help you tell your story.

Let's collaborate!
