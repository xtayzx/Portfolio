Overview

The Usual is a student short film final project created for a film and media class. Contains some coarse language, viewer discretion is advised.

Big thank you to Chewie's Biscuit Co. for making this film possible.

In the era of online dating, a man takes several ‘matches’ to his favourite diner with no success. A certain waiter observes from the sidelines.
