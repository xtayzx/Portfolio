Project Overview

Meta Meme is an image mosaic application created as a final project for an computational image and media coding class. Inspired by the popular children's series Spongebob Squarepants, we wanted to base our application on the process of taking small images composed of the character Squidward Tentacles and composing larger images from a composition of smaller images.
